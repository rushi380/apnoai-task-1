Who we are

About DevLaunch

A community built by students, for students — where learning is practical, feedback is honest, and nobody builds alone.

DevLaunch began as a small group of students meeting after class to figure out why their code was not working. That study group grew into a community of learners who believe the best way to understand technology is to build with it.

We learn by doing. Members follow a practical path from HTML and CSS fundamentals to interactive JavaScript, then apply that knowledge in team projects that look strong in a portfolio and feel real in an interview.

What makes DevLaunch different is the people. Senior students review your code, mentors help you get unstuck, and everyone shares what they learn. No gatekeeping — just steady progress and a team that wants you to win.
